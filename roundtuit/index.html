To-do list for Android

Roundtuit

The to-do list for jobs you'll get to eventually. Keep your own list, share one with the people you live with, and let them ask you for things. Eventually you'll get a roundtuit.

▶Coming soon toGoogle Play

What it does

  • 1

    Everything has a place

    Sort jobs into categories like Home, Garden or Errands, then add your own. Each task can carry a due date, notes, cost and time spent.

  • 2

    Chores that come back

    Set a task to repeat every Saturday or on the 15th of each month, and a fresh copy shows up on its day. Unfinished copies stay on the list, so nothing quietly disappears.

  • 3

    Reminders when you want them

    Any task with a due date can remind you at the time you choose, and a reminder goes away once the task is done.

  • 4

    Lists you share

    Share a list with the people you live with, and everyone sees the same jobs and who checked them off. Each person uses their own phone and their own account.

  • 5

    Let people ask

    Give someone the right to ask you for things. Their requests land on your list in the category you picked, and they can see when each one is done.

  • 6

    See what you got done

    The Metrics tab adds up what you've added and finished, plus the cost and time spent, by category, list or person.

  • 7

    Ask your own AI about it (coming soon)

    Connect the AI app you already use, then ask it what is overdue, or tell it to add a job or check one off. It makes changes only when you say so, and you can see everything it read and did.

Free, with a couple of extras

  • FreeThe whole list on your phone, with no ads and no account.
  • Daily Backup (a one-time purchase)A backup to your own Google Drive every day, and whenever you ask.
  • Sync (monthly or yearly)Use Roundtuit on all your devices, your phone and tablet alike, with your list in step between them. Sync also lets you make shared lists and invite people to ask you for things. Coming soon with Sync: connect your own AI app, so you can talk to it about your list and it can make changes when you say so. Includes Daily Backup.

Your data

Your tasks and categories are kept on your phone. If you turn on Daily Backup, a copy goes to your own Google Drive.

If you choose Sync, your list is kept in Roundtuit's online database so it follows you to a new phone and your devices all match. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.
