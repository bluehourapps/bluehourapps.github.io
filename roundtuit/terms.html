Your list

The tasks you add are yours. On a shared list, its members can see and change the tasks on it, and the list's owner can remove members. How your data is handled is described in the privacy policy.

Plans

Roundtuit is free to use on your phone. Daily Backup is a one-time purchase, and Roundtuit Sync is a monthly or yearly subscription. Google Play handles every purchase, and its refund and cancellation rules apply. If Sync ends, your list moves to your phone and keeps working on Free.

Reminders and backups

Reminders depend on your phone's notification settings and on Google's notification service, and may come late or not at all. Daily Backup depends on your Google Drive. Don't rely on Roundtuit for anything time-critical.
