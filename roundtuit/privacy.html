Roundtuit: Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-28

Roundtuit is a to-do list app for Android, made by Blue Hour. This page says what it keeps, where, and how to delete it.

What it collects

  • The tasks you enter: what the task is, who requested it and when, its category, its due date and reminder, how often it repeats, its status, notes, cost and time spent.
  • Your preferences (display name, gender, birthday month and year, theme, reminder times, time zone, which fields show and how the list is sorted).

On the Free plan your list stays on your phone only, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Google sign-in and your Drive (optional)

  • You don't have to sign in. Roundtuit works fully on your phone without an account. Roundtuit asks you to sign in with Google only when you choose Daily Backup or Roundtuit Sync, or accept an invite someone sent you.
  • Signing in gives Roundtuit your Google account's name and email address, used only to sign you in and keep your list yours, and permission to the files Roundtuit creates in your Drive (Google's drive.file permission). It can't see your other files, your Gmail or your contacts.
  • With Daily Backup, Roundtuit saves a copy of your list to a folder it makes in your own Google Drive, every day and whenever you ask. That copy is yours: you can delete it there at any time.

Roundtuit Sync and sharing (optional)

  • With Roundtuit Sync, your list is stored in Roundtuit's online database, where only your own signed-in account can read or change your rows, and a copy is kept on your phone so the list shows offline.
  • If you share: the names you give, the email address of anyone you invite (until they accept), and the tasks on the lists you share or request from. Tasks on a shared list can be seen and changed by that list's members.
  • If you turn on reminders while signed in: a notification address for your phone (from Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging) and the app's version, used only to send your reminders and sharing alerts.
  • When Sync ends, your list moves to your phone and the server copy is kept until the end of the next full calendar month, so it can come back if you return, and is then deleted.

What it doesn't do

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for any other purpose. No ads, analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Data → Wipe all data clears your list from the phone and, if you are signed in, from the server. Delete account does that and also removes your Roundtuit account. You can also ask for deletion by emailing privacy@bluehourapps.dev. To remove Roundtuit's access to your Google account: Google Account → Security → Third-party access → Roundtuit → Remove access.
