Lattice

A health tracker for Android, from Blue Hour

Lattice keeps your workouts, food, weight, sleep, lab results and more in one place, on your phone. There's no account on someone else's server and nothing to sync to a company database. Your data lives on your phone, and backups go to your own Google Drive.

What you get

  • Free: the whole app on your phone, with no ads.
  • Daily Backup (a one-time purchase): a backup to your own Google Drive every day, and whenever you ask.
  • Lattice Connect (monthly or yearly): connect Lattice to your own AI app, so you can ask it about your data. When it suggests a change, nothing happens until you approve it there.

Why Google sign-in

Signing in with Google lets Lattice save its backup in your Google Drive. Lattice asks Google only for the files it creates itself. It can't see anything else in your Drive, your Gmail or your contacts.

Who makes it

Blue Hour is a small collection of apps designed by one person, from a laptop at home, after hours. I wanted apps that work the way I need them to: no ads, no selling data, and a free version that's the real app.
