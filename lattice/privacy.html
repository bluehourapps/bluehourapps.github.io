Lattice: Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-21

Short version: your health data stays on your phone, and in your own Google Drive only if you choose Daily Backup. I don't run a database that holds your health data. The only things Lattice ever sends to my server are the feedback notes you choose to send, below. I don't sell anything about you, and there are no ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.

On your phone

Everything you log or connect (workouts, food, weight, sleep, lab results, readings from Health Connect and the rest) is stored on your phone. Uninstalling Lattice or using Delete All Data in Settings removes it.

Android's own backup (optional)

Android can back up apps to your own Google account. This is set in your phone's settings, not in Lattice. When it's on, Android copies only a few small app settings files that Android keeps for Lattice to your Google account, and puts them back if you reinstall Lattice or move to a new phone. It never copies your health records, Lattice's database, photos or any other files. Lattice can't see that copy. Your AI keys and sign-in tokens are left out of it.

Google sign-in and your Drive (optional)

  • You don't have to sign in. Lattice works fully on your phone without an account. Lattice asks you to sign in with Google only when you choose Daily Backup or Lattice Connect.
  • Signing in gives Lattice your Google account's name, email address and account id, and permission to the files Lattice creates in your Drive (Google's drive.file permission). It can't see your other files, your Gmail or your contacts.
  • With Daily Backup, Lattice saves a copy of its data to your Drive every day and whenever you ask. That copy is yours: it sits in your Drive, and you can delete it there at any time.

Lattice Connect (optional)

  • When you add Lattice to your AI app, a small connector run by Blue Hour on Google Cloud reads your Lattice backup in your Drive each time your AI app asks for it, and passes it to your AI app. The connector keeps no copy of your data and has no database. Its logs hold only codes, counts and timings.
  • What your AI app does with what it reads is covered by that app's own privacy policy.
  • If your AI app suggests a change, nothing changes until you approve it there. Lattice then applies it on your phone. To let Lattice know, the connector sends your phone a silent signal through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. The signal carries no health data, only "there's a change waiting".
  • You can disconnect at any time, in your AI app or in your Google account's third-party access settings.

Other things that leave the phone

  • AI helpers, optional: if you save your own key for an AI company (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) and ask Lattice to read a nutrition label, a supplement label or a lab-result screenshot, that one photo goes straight from your phone to that provider. Nothing else goes with it, and Lattice doesn't keep the photo. To pick each company's current model, Lattice reads a short list of model names from this website; that request sends nothing about you.
  • Food search: typed search words go to USDA FoodData Central, and a scanned barcode number goes to Open Food Facts. No diary entries are sent.
  • Purchases: Google Play handles payment, and I never see your card. RevenueCat, the service that tracks which plan you have, gets your purchase records and your Google account id.

Feedback (optional)

When you use Send feedback in About Lattice, your message, the topic you picked, the email you typed if you want a reply, and the app version, plan, phone model and Android version go to my server and to my inbox, so I can read and answer it. With feedback I also receive the gender you chose in your profile and your age range (for example 25–34), never your birth date. Only with feedback. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. Feedback is kept as correspondence; to have yours deleted, email privacy@bluehourapps.dev.

Diagnostics report (optional)

Only when you pick "Something's not working" and tick "Send a diagnostics report with this feedback", Lattice also sends a technical log of the last hour: which screens opened, which buttons were tapped, and any errors the app hit. It never includes your health data, anything you typed, your email or your keys. It's tied to a random id this phone made, not to you or your Google account. It's kept until you use Delete All Data in Settings, which deletes it from my server too, or until you ask at privacy@bluehourapps.dev.

Feedback and diagnostics reports are stored with Supabase, in the United States.

What I don't do

No ads, no selling or sharing data for advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, no location tracking. The diagnostics report above is sent only when you ask, with a note you write.

Your choices

  • View, export or delete everything in the app, under Settings.
  • Delete the backup in your Google Drive.
  • Delete your diagnostics reports: Delete All Data in Settings.
  • Remove Lattice's access to your Google account: Google Account → Security → Third-party access → Lattice → Remove access.

Children

Lattice is meant for people 18 and older.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: privacy@bluehourapps.dev
