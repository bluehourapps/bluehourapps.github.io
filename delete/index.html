Delete your account and data

Last updated 2026-11-02

This page covers every Blue Hour app: Echo, Lattice, Roots, Roundtuit and Rainquail. Each app's account is its own, so deleting it in one app never touches another app's data.

In the app

  1. Open the app and go to Settings → Data.
  2. In the Danger zone, tap Delete account and confirm.

This shows only when you're signed in. It deletes your account in that app and everything the app keeps for it on its server, straight away.

If you never signed in, nothing of yours is kept on a server: your data is only on your phone. Settings → Data → Wipe all data clears it, and so does uninstalling the app.

By email

If you can't use the app, email privacy@bluehourapps.dev from the Google account you signed in with. Say which app it's for. Your account and its data are deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply once it's done.

If you never signed in but sent feedback, you can also ask for that feedback to be deleted. Say which app, roughly when you sent it, and the email address you gave, if any.

What gets deleted

  • Your account in that app, and its plan record.
  • Everything the app keeps for you on its server: your synced data, the app's settings and profile, feedback you sent while signed in, and any diagnostics reports sent with it.
  • Any AI app's access to your account through the app's connector, and the connector's activity list.

What stays

  • Backups in your own Google Drive stay there until you delete them. They're your files.
  • Purchase records kept by Google Play, which Google holds under its own policies.
